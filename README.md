# Spatial-Immune-Tetrad-Analysis

**Codes and datasets accompanying the study:**

> **Spatial multicellular immune tetrads formed by CD4 T cells, CD8 T cells, DC1 cells, and macrophages in the tumor microenvironment**

## Overview

**Spatial-Immune-Tetrad-Analysis** contains the computational workflows and datasets used to characterize coordinated multicellular immune interactions within the tumor microenvironment.

The study focuses on spatially organized immune **tetrads** composed of:

- CD4 T cells
- CD8 T cells
- DC1 cells
- Macrophages

Rather than examining individual cell populations or pairwise cell–cell interactions in isolation, the analytical framework investigates the coordinated spatial organization of these four immune populations as multicellular interaction units.

The repository contains workflows for identifying immune tetrads from spatially resolved single-cell data, quantifying their abundance and spatial organization, and evaluating their associations with biological and clinical features.
